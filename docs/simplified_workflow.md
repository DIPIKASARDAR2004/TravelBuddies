# JourneyPilot: Simplified User Journey

This document outlines the core user experiences in JourneyPilot, designed for presentations or creating high-level flowcharts. 

---

## 1. User Onboarding & Setup
*How a new user gets started on the platform.*
- **Step 1: Sign Up** (Choose Personal, Corporate, or Host account)
- **Step 2: Profile Setup** (Add personal details and emergency contacts)
- **Step 3: Dashboard** (Access all travel tools from the main hub)

## 2. Smart Trip Planning
*How a user generates a customized travel itinerary.*
- **Step 1: Enter Preferences** (Input destination, travel dates, budget, and travel style)
- **Step 2: AI Generation** (System generates a day-by-day smart itinerary)
- **Step 3: Save & Share** (Save the itinerary to the profile and share with travel buddies)

## 3. Booking Engine (Hotels, Buses, Trains)
*How a user reserves their travel logistics.*
- **Step 1: Search** (Input locations and dates for Hotel, Bus, or Train)
- **Step 2: Compare Options** (View available options, prices, and reviews)
- **Step 3: Confirm Secure Booking** (Select an option, make a payment via the Protected Escrow system, and receive confirmation. Funds are held safely until check-in.)

## 4. Women Safety Module
*How a user accesses emergency and safety features.*
- **Step 1: Activate Module** (Enter the dedicated safety section)
- **Step 2: Live Tracking** (View current location on offline-capable maps)
- **Step 3: Take Action** 
  - **Option A:** Press SOS to instantly alert emergency contacts and authorities.
  - **Option B:** Click "Find Safe Stay" to locate verified, highly-rated secure hotels nearby.

## 5. Travel Utilities & Budgeting
*How a user manages their trip on the go.*
- **Step 1: Open Utilities** (Access the tools section during a trip)
- **Step 2: Track Expenses** (Log daily expenses and split bills with travel buddies)
- **Step 3: Conversions** (Use the built-in currency converter for international trips)

---

## The Tech Stack (High-Level overview)
*(For the hexagon graphic on your slide)*
- **Frontend / Interface**: Next.js & React (What the user sees and interacts with)
- **Backend Booking Logic**: Next.js API Routes & Server Actions (The engine that processes bookings)
- **User Database & Auth**: Supabase (Securely stores user profiles and itineraries)
- **Maps & Location**: Leaflet (Provides mapping and offline location tracking)

---

## Innovation
*What makes JourneyPilot unique in the market.*
- **AI-Powered Planning**: Replaces hours of manual research with instant, dynamic day-by-day smart itineraries tailored to user preferences.
- **Integrated Safety Ecosystem**: Uniquely bundles a dedicated Women Safety Module (SOS alerts, verified safe stays, offline tracking) directly into a core travel booking platform.
- **All-In-One Consolidation**: Eliminates the need for multiple apps by combining trip planning, logistics booking, mapping, and budget tracking (bill splitting/currency conversion) into one unified experience.

## Impact and Benefits
*The value JourneyPilot delivers to its users.*
- **Enhanced Safety & Peace of Mind**: Empowers solo and vulnerable travelers to explore with confidence through real-time tracking and emergency features.
- **Massive Time Savings**: Reduces the friction of travel preparation from days to seconds.
- **Seamless Financial Management**: Prevents group travel disputes through transparent expense logging and instant bill splitting.
- **Simplified Travel**: Lowers the barrier to entry for travel, encouraging more people to explore safely and efficiently.
